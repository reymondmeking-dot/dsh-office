# Office report writing

1. Open with a 3-5 line summary: what was done, the key result, what is still open.
2. Body sections follow the reader's questions, not the order you worked in.
3. Every claim has evidence: a file path, a link, a number, or "inferred".
4. Use tables for comparisons and lists for steps; keep paragraphs under 5 lines.
5. End with "Next steps" (owner + action) and "Known limitations".
6. Write in the language of the task (Chinese task → Chinese report).
